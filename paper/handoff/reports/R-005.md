## Answer

On the session-isolated L07 replay, current DP8 reaches 8.91 good req/s under
the Standard SLO (0.81× July's 10.99) and July's batched-token limit (32768)
does not help (7.90, 0.72×). R-002's DP8 number (5.69) came from a grid that
skipped DP8's knee (C8 then C18); with C12/C16 included, DP8's Standard
capacity is 8.91. DP8 is ITL-bound: ITL P95 47 ms at C12, 69 ms at C16, 85 ms
at C20, while TTFT P95 stays ≤1.5 s.

`max_num_seqs` 256 in both. Only the batched-token limit was varied.

18.45/10.12 = 1.82 (R-002 reported 2.94 / 3.24 / 1.92). PAP/PD is unchanged.
