## Answer (E1)

On native-32K multi-turn traffic (s240), PAP 7PA1P sustains the highest
maximum D2 goodput: 3.63 [3.59, 3.67] good req/s vs 3.00 [2.96, 3.03] for PD
6P2D (+21%), 2.32 for DP8 (+56%) and 2.14 for PD 4P4D (+70%). The ordering
holds under D4 and for every D2 scaling from 0.75× to 2×. PAP is TTFT-limited;
6P2D approaches the ITL limit (ITL P95 69–74 ms at C30–C40) while PAP stays at
≤ 65 ms P95 at every load; DP8 is ITL-bound from C20.

## Validity and limitations

- Feasibility uses mean attainment over runs (COMMON §2.4). July's stricter
  "every run passes" rule gives the same knees: per-run D2 attainment is
  96.5–97.6% (PAP C40), 98.3–99.2% (6P2D C30), 95.8–99.0% (4P4D C20) and
  97.6–98.9% (DP8 C10).
- Throughput and goodput CIs are run-level bootstraps (n = 3 or 5).

## Unexpected findings / suggested follow-ups

- DP8 has the highest raw request rate at high load (3.48 req/s at C60) but
  almost none of it meets D2; the paper should show both throughput and
  goodput so this is not misread.
